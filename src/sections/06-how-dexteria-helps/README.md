# Solution: meet the Smart Ball (Raika 04)

## What it shows
The solution heading and an interactive simulation: press and hold the ball (or Space) and keep the marker in the zone for 5 seconds. Four measures are listed beside it.

## Source

The wording is the teammate's and is unchanged.
