The problem

Our hands hold our independence. Getting them back happens mostly at home, alone.

Holding a cup. Fastening a button. Turning a key. After a stroke, a hand injury or with fine motor difficulties, the smallest tasks become the hardest. Recovery needs daily practice, and almost all of it happens at home.

What goes wrong at home

Three gaps in recovery today

  1. 01The gap

    Too little guided practice

    A few hours of therapy each week. The rest happens at home, with no one to guide it.

  2. 02The blind spot

    No record between visits

    Between visits, all the therapist has to go on is what the patient remembers.

  3. 03The drop-off

    Progress you can’t see

    Is the hand getting stronger? Without numbers, nobody knows, so exercises never change and motivation fades.

    One small ball closes all three gaps.

One app, three ways to use it

  • For patients

    Practice that feels like a game

    Squeeze-controlled games, spoken instructions and big buttons. Progress shown as a % of your other hand.

  • For clinicians

    Real numbers between visits

    See grip, tremor and adherence, set goals and difficulty, and share reports, with the patient’s consent.

  • For caregivers

    Peace of mind at home

    Know if today’s session is done, get missed-session alerts and send gentle reminders.
